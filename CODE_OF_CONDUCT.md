# Code of conduct

Contributors and maintainers must keep project spaces respectful, constructive,
and free from harassment. Disagreement about designs and trade-offs is welcome;
personal attacks, intimidation, discriminatory language, and publication of
private information are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms the project or its community. Report conduct concerns privately
to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with
appropriate confidentiality.
